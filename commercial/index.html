Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse at peak service. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Heavy-Use Systems

Hot Water for Hotels, Apartments, and Large Facilities

In a hotel, apartment building, or large facility, hot water demand runs around the clock, with big morning peaks when dozens of showers run at once, and the scale of the load calls for the right experience and a structured plan. Newton Water Heater handles high-demand commercial systems, sending experienced technicians to service banks of commercial tanks, racked tankless systems, indirect storage tanks, and recirculation loops built for continuous operation.

Downtime in a large building is measured in lost revenue and tenant complaints, so our approach is built around prevention. That means full system assessments, sizing based on real occupancy, redundant equipment so a single failure never cuts off hot water, recirculation service, and routine flushing and descaling to hold capacity. When something does fail, our repair team isolates it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, verify proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Newton and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Newton Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
